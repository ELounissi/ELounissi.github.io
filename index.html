open-ml-notebooks

A free book of 86 executed Jupyter notebooks covering machine learning, deep learning and reinforcement learning, one method per notebook. Each chapter derives the maths, implements the method from scratch, then measures where it wins and where it loses on shared open datasets. No number appears in the text unless the notebook printed it.

Computer-Vision

Shape descriptors explained and tested on 360 rotations of the letter E and multiple transformations of the photo of Lena. Raw, central, normalized and Hu moments reveal which descriptors resist translation, scale and rotation. Reproducible scripts, numerical checks and several results demonstrate mathematical invariance.

Machine Learning and Dimensionality Reduction for Plant Health

Code of the paper "Impact of dimensionality reduction on predictions of agricultural crop health using machine learning and deep learning". Plant-health prediction comparing eight ML/DL models across five Dimensionality Reduction methods. Three vegetable crops and two tasks : disease-occurrence classification and disease-severity regression. Results, rankings, tables and figures are included.
